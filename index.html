This account may look like another profile to some people. But to me, it's years of memories. ❤️

Lately, I've been changing. Not in a bad way. Just growing. 😃

Growth often happens quietly — while nobody is watching, while nobody is clapping, while nobody even notices.

I've learned that not everyone who knows your name understands your journey, and not everyone around you belongs in your future. 🌙

This isn't goodbye. This is simply me choosing my future.
